Virtual
Design Kit

Build a sheet-metal object in VR by snapping parts together and the software writes the laser-cutting stencil while you do it. It comes back flat, ready to fold.

Read the Hochparterre article

A lamp designed in VR, cut flat by a local shop, folded by hand.

Role
Exhibition environment, physical kit design and production, 3D modelling of the kit elements
Context
Tobias Kappeler, BA Thesis, 2019, HyperWerk FHNW
Built with
VR, Blender, sheet metal, laser cutting

Why it matters

Designing for fabrication normally means CAD skills, unfolding by hand, and a long wait to find out whether the thing is the size you imagined. In VR you design at full scale, with your hands, and the stencil is a by-product rather than a second job.

What came out of it

A working design-to-fabrication loop, VR to laser cutter to folded object
Real objects, lamps and furniture, built by people with no CAD experience
