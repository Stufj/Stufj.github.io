Remote Play
Experiments

Nobody wanted another meeting tool. So I spent a semester asking what if a video call were a space you could play in and built three answers.

Tagging Tentacle: Playing together through a Zoom-Call.

Role
Concept, electronics, patching and documentation for all three
Constraint
Through COVID-19 everybody has to stay in their own room, and nobody installs anything
Built with
Max/MSP, Arduino, OBS, Scratch, Zoom, Miro

Why it matters

Remote tools optimise for talking and screen-sharing, which is why remote groups stop being playful. Play needs a shared space and a body in it — both of which a grid of faces removes.

Each prototype smuggles one of them back in using only tools people already had open: a webcam, a browser, a call.

What came out of it

Three working artefacts, each playable over a standard video call
Refreshing interactions during COVID-19 online meetings.
The insight that hacking a system does not need special equipment.
