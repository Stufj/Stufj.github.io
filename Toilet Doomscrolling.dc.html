Toilet
Doomscrolling

A toilet paper roll and a TikTok feed that scrolls exactly as far as you pull it.

The idea

How much of our life disappears into doomscrolling, and where do we actually do most of it?

The mechanism

A computer mouse hidden behind the holder turns with the paper, and its scroll wheel drives an endless TikTok feed on the phone above.

The setting

Shown alongside other work at “Where do you lose your time?”, Basel Art Center, 2026.

The long version

This started as one of the eight mouse based prototypes from my thesis, and became a full scale piece for a group show. Pull the paper, and the feed scrolls with it, forever, since a refill stack sits right next to the piece. The joke lands harder as an installation than it ever could as a sketch, because you see the amount of paper you wasted for every reel you watched.

Part of Un-Useless Interactions, the same project that produced the free workshop format at un-useless.art.
