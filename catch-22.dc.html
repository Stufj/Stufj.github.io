catch-22

A live performance for an audience that could not be in the room, streamed as its own medium, not as a substitute for a stage.

Coordinating catch-22, live and on camera at the same time.

Role
Concept, performance, live visuals, technical direction
With
Fabio Bissinger, bachelor thesis, HyperWerk FHNW
Built with
OBS, projection, live camera, a built set

Why it matters

The performance happened during the COVID-19 restrictions. The goal was to create an intimate performance and break the barrier between the virtual and the real world. The audience was both spectator and part of the performance.

What I did

Performed on camera, created visuals and aniamtion and ran the live visual mix in the same take
Coordinated the team and the production schedule
Built and operated the whole technical chain.
OBS, projection, sound
