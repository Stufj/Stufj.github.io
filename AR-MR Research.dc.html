I build augmented-reality prototypes that let people see a proposed change to their own city before it is built — and run the workshops where they argue about it.

Why it matters

Plans for public space are argued over in floor plans and jargon, so the people who will live with the result are the least equipped to take part. Holding a phone up to an empty square and seeing the proposal standing there changes who can have an opinion.
