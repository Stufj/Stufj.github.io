VR
Parcours

A VR course you walk through for real. Wooden beams, foam mats and thin boards under the floor mean you feel the virtual world with your feet.

Role
Concept, 3D environments, VR build, physical set
Context
OpenHouse 2020, HyperWerk FHNW — public, one visitor at a time
Built with
Unreal Engine 4, Blender, Oculus Quest, timber

Why it matters

Room-scale VR usually asks you to believe in a world your feet know is a flat studio floor. Putting a real beam where the virtual beam is, adding fans, placing foam on the floor and position thin boards on the right place costs almost nothing and does more for presence than another million polygons.

What came out of it

A walkable course that ran all day at a public open house
A cheap, repeatable recipe for haptic floors under VR
An immersive experience that tricks your brain and body
